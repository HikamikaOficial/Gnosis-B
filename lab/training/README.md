# GNOSIS Training Lab

Prepared for future local/self-owned model training when evidence shows it is worthwhile.

Potential targets:
- routing classifiers;
- risk classifiers;
- failure attribution;
- retrieval/reranking;
- local reviewer/worker models;
- trajectory distillation.

Do not train just because training is possible.

Every experiment requires dataset provenance, baseline, held-out evaluation, regression and rollback.

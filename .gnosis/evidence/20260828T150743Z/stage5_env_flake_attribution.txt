The rate is the same on both sides of the change, so the change is not
the cause. Three independent lines of evidence agree:

 1. ALREADY ON THE RECORD. docs/NEXT_ACTIONS.md has referred to "the
    pre-existing work-queue flake" since 2026-08-26, and the ADR-0026
    baseline is stated as "874 tests and 60 subtests WHEN THE FLAKY
    WORK-QUEUE TEST COOPERATES". Stage 5 did not introduce it.

 2. REPRODUCED ON BOTH TREES at the same rate, above.

 3. ROOT CAUSE IS IN THE TEST'S OWN FIXTURE, not the product.
    TestRepairsFromTheIndependentReview._short_lived() builds the
    WorkAuthority with default_ttl_s=0.05. The test then performs three
    claim/release cycles, each writing JSON to disk, and every one of
    them must finish inside 50 ms of wall clock or the lease expires and
    release() raises StaleLeaseError - which is exactly the observed
    failure. The test is about ATTEMPT EXHAUSTION, not lease expiry, so
    it never needed a short TTL at all; its neighbour
    test_a_brief_whose_worker_died_becomes_claimable_again does need one
    and deliberately sleeps 0.15 s.

SCOPE DISCIPLINE. The repair is one line - give this test a normal-TTL
queue - but tests/test_work_queue.py is outside the scope this stage was
authorised to touch, and editing a test during a closure gate is exactly
the move a reviewer should be able to reject. IT WAS NOT MODIFIED. The
repair is recorded in NEXT_ACTIONS 0-XXIII for separate authorisation,
with its own before/after measurement.

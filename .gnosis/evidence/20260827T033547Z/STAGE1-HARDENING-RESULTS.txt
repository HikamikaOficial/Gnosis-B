5. A HOLE IN THE CLOSED-WORLD MODEL ITSELF, FOUND AND CLOSED
  A load-time closure cannot see an import written INSIDE A FUNCTION BODY, so
  the whole model could be bypassed by moving the dependency there. Added an
  AST scan of src/gnosis/trust/** that resolves every internal import at any
  nesting level and refuses anything neither allowlisted nor declared in
  DEFERRED_TCB_EXPANSION. The scan is proven non-vacuous by
  test_the_deferred_expansion_list_is_not_stale, which requires the declared
  lazy dependency to actually be found in the source.

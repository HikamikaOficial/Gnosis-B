---
name: gnosis-proof-packet
description: Build an evidence packet proving a GNOSIS task or milestone satisfies its acceptance criteria.
---

Map every acceptance criterion to evidence.

Include when applicable:
- task/spec hash;
- base/result commits;
- diff summary;
- changed symbols;
- exact commands;
- test/build results;
- output/artifact hashes;
- review findings and dispositions;
- policy/security verdicts;
- benchmark/performance deltas;
- dependency changes;
- unresolved dissent;
- final promotion decision.

Never use “looks good” as proof.
